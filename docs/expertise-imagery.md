# Expertise Service Imagery

Created September 21, 2026 for Bryan’s request to match Jackson Family Dental’s full-width photo service grid.

## Provenance And Use

Seven original images were generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool, one call per asset. No Jackson media was downloaded or reused. Generated people, teeth, dental models, and surroundings are illustrative; they do not document CIDWP patients, treatment results, equipment, or premises. No branded products or treatment guarantees are depicted. The existing `clinical-care.jpg` and `practice-interior.jpg` are used for General Dentistry and Sedation Options respectively; their sources remain documented in [Round 2 sources](round2-asset-sources.md).

The generated source PNGs are stored in `src/assets/expertise/`. Astro produces responsive WebP versions at build time. Images are decorative inside service links; the visible Title Case service name provides the accessible link label. The section uses three columns on desktop, two on tablets, and one on phones, with dark gradients, thin white dividers, hover treatment, keyboard focus indicators, and reduced-motion support.

## Final Prompts And Files

Validation: `astro check` completed with zero errors, warnings, or hints; the production build completed for `/` and `/review/`. Browser checks at 320, 390, 768, 1024, 1440, and 1920 pixels confirmed nine loaded images, readable labels within their tiles, and no horizontal overflow. Keyboard focus, reduced-motion handling, and the review route were also checked. QA captures and the report are under `.loop/expertise/` (ignored by Git).
